## Enjoy your music this November

Whether you want to [Make Music in Bingley]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link regular-activities.md %}), [Enjoy Live Music in Bingley]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link live/this-month.md %}), or Enjoy Live Music elsewhere in Bradford, then there is plenty to do this November as Bradford celebrates Music Month.

There are a couple of other websites that are doing a great job of tracking what's on at the moment: 
<a  href="https://www.bradfordmusicscene.com/">Bradford Music Scene <i class="fa fa-external-link" aria-hidden="true"></i></a>, <a href="https://itsoninbradford.co.uk/events-category/music-month/" class="fs-3">What's On In Bradford <i class="fa fa-external-link" aria-hidden="true"></i></a>, and of course the <a href="https://bradfordculture.co.uk/programme/bradford-music-month/">Bradford Culture Company <i class="fa fa-external-link" aria-hidden="true"></i></a> so there are no excuses!

## In Bingley ...

From Traditional Music at the Chip N Ern, The Brown Cow, and now the Royal at Crossflatts, to indie alternative music at the Rumble events, to a showcase of local talent at our Sounds In Town Events, singers and songwriters at First Monday Folk, Upstairs at the Chip N Ern, and in the Library, to our Winter Concert featuring Pop Up Brass, to ticketed events at The Bingley Arts Centre, there's plenty on.

And that's before you even look at all the groups, classes, teachers and events that will help you make music if you want to.

Follow the links above and check out our Facebook and Instagram pages for updates.
