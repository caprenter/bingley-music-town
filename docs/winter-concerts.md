## Winter Concerts 2026-27

Following another summer of great live music on the Bandstand in [Myrtle Park]({% link _venues/myrtle_park.md %}) we're extending the concerts into the Winter with a series of Free/Affordable family friendly music in [Bingley Arts Centre<i class="fa fa-external-link" aria-hidden="true"></i>](https://www.bingleyartscentre.co.uk/).

Funded by [Bingley Town Council<i class="fa fa-external-link" aria-hidden="true"></i>](https://www.bingleytowncouncil.gov.uk/), subsidised by [Bingley Arts Centre<i class="fa fa-external-link" aria-hidden="true"></i>](https://www.bingleyartscentre.co.uk/), and supported by Bingley Music Town volunteers, we've added 5 more events to the ever expanding musical [What's On<i class="fa fa-external-link" aria-hidden="true"></i>](https://www.bingleyartscentre.co.uk/category/music) Offer at the Arts Centre.

We've built a programme mixing in our free events (donations welcome!) with events that were already in place over the winter, some of which are ticketed, so check the listings or check with the venue if unsure.
